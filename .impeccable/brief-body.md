# Surface brief: el tablón (index.html)

Scope: the whole SPA, mode **Operate**. One screen (the 3×3 board) plus a full-screen cell editor and a share sheet.
Audience: family members of every age on a phone, some with no app experience. Task: fill nine cells (up to three images each; colour cell = up to three colours), then generate and share one image of the board with their name.
Constraints: fixed cell order; Google image search via Programmable Search Element once a cx is configured, a free image bank meanwhile, device photo always available; everything saved on the device; no backend.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The board is a Nochebuena bingo card. Nine numbered squares you mark one by one until you call ¡BINGO! and share it; it refuses the category default of white rounded cards with a Christmas-red accent and snowflakes.

OWN-WORLD: Cobalt bingo ink (#2234a8) owns the shell; the card is pure white paper printed in that one ink plus black, no greys. Heavy extra-condensed grotesque numerals (Archivo, width axis) printed huge in each square; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for every sentence. Fluorescent magenta dauber dot (#ff2e88, multiply) marks a finished square; marigold (#ffd21f) is the call to action only. «Lo que NO regalarme» photos carry a black rubber «NO» stamp. Every photo cropped square at one scale.

STORY: Visitor sees a bingo card with their name on it, understands each square is a question, fills them in order guided by the next-step sign, calls ¡BINGO! and posts the image in the family chat.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt band: «Amigo invisible», year, progress «4 de 9». The name field lives in the card head as the printed blank «CARTÓN DE ____», like the line on a paper cartón; the shared image names the card the same way. Next-step sign under it: «Ahora: 5 · Pequeño lujo diario» tapping opens that square. Below, the white card fills the width: 3×3 ruled squares, number large in ink, label beneath; filled squares become a 2×2 of equal squares: the printed number with the dauber over it, then up to three photos (or colours) at one scale. When 9/9, a marigold «¡BINGO! Crear mi imagen» replaces the sign at the top.

FORM: Cartón de bingo de Nochebuena, position 6 of 7 on the grounded list. Seed key c046497c. Raises: airport wayfinding (next empty square always signed), vertical feed (editor owns the screen, one job), ikeda (two-ink discipline), ticket wallet (NO stamp cancels, never hides), botanical folio (one square crop for every photo). Signature move: the dauber stamp landing on a square's number when it is completed, and the ¡BINGO! call when the card is full.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
